*Climb up the wall to escape the cat!*

### CONTROLS

* Reach : move mouse
* Left click/A key : Grab hold with left hand
* Right click/D key : Grab hold with right hand

### How to play

- Use both arms to scale the wall.
- Quickly scale the wall to escape the cat
- The side bar shows how close you are to the top, and how clsoe the cat is to catching you
