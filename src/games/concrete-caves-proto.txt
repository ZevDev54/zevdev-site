# Warning! This prototype has very poor performance, it will only run well on a good computer! 
*Explore a mysterious randomly generated concrete world*

### CONTROLS

* Move with WASD
* Look around using the mouse
* Left click on blue crystals to collect them

### How to play

- Explore the concrete caves and collect blue crystals
- A super basic prototype where I was testing out simple random generated levels and interesting atmospheres.

### Reflection

- I think that randomly generated levels are pretty neat, and offer interesting exploration oppourtunities.
- A multiplayer version of this with more sophisticated movement (ex. ropes) would be pretty neat!
