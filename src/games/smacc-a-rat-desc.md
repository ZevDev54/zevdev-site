*Smack the rat. Buy more rats. Improve your smacking power. Get an ungodly score!*

### CONTROLS

* Left Click: Smack! (and buy upgrades)

### How to Play

- Smack the rat to accumulate points.
- Buy a better smack to have a bigger smack radius and gain 1 extra point per level of smack.
- Buy more rats, so that you can smack multiple at once!
- Keep buying rats until you get the super rare pink and green rat variants.
- Try to see as many rat names as possible! My sister named most of them.

### Reflection

* This was just a silly one day project, but it truned out to be pretty fun and addicting.
* Some of my friends got scores in the upper millions, which is pretty ridiculous but shows how addictive "number go up" games can be!
