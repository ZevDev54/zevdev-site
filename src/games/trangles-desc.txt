*Watch the Trangles in their natural habitat as they grow, fight and breed.*

### CONTROLS

* Q : Clear Trangles (make sure there aren't too many trangles, it might crash your computer lol)
* R : Spawn moe Trangles

### How to Play

- There is no interaction in this simple simulator! Just watch.
- Trangles will multiply if they touch a like-colored trangle 3 times.
- Trangles will die if they touch 3 opposite-colored trangles.
- Trangles grow slightly over time.

### Reflection

* Gave me an appreciation for how simple rules make complex behaviour
* I later did experiments with Boids. I think some sort of unit command game that uses boids would be very neat.
* I want to make another simple simulator like this. I really enjoy watching the trangles fill up the screen. Often A dominant color will rise.
