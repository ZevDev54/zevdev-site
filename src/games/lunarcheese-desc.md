*Defend the cheese-mining robots from deadly corruption with your trusty broom!*

### CONTROLS

* WASD/arrow keys : Move
* Left click : Sweep

### How to play

- Sweep the red corruptions to prevent them from infecting and destroying your mining robots.
- The robots will mine cheese and deliver it to the collector-bot.
- When the cheese meter is filled, your mining operation will be provided with a new random robot to increase your efficiency.
- You can help your robots by sweeping the cheese into the collector-bot
