*Parkour through an endless amount of colorful levels!*

### CONTROLS

* Move with WASD
* Look around using the mouse
* Left click on golden block to beat level
* R to restart

### How to play

- Parkour through colorful, randomly generated levels
- Reach the gold block and click it to beat the level
- Repeat... forever!

### Reflection

- This is one of the most interesting games from my early work.
- Gave me an interest in randomly generated levels! This concept could definetly be expanded upon.
