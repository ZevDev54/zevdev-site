*Survive waves of burnt toast, jumping jams, butter cubes, and jam tanks in this fast-paced twin-stick breakfast shooter!*


### CONTROLS

* Aim with mouse / right stick
* Move with WASD / left stick
* Shoot with Space or Left Click / right trigger
* Pickup weapons with E key / left controller button
* Pause with P
* Exit with Backspace


### How to Play

- Avoid getting hit by enemies
- Gain score from killing enemies
- Try out different weapons and use their different strengths to your advantage
- Try to survive all 20 waves!
