*Damage the package then deliver it to get fired and receive sweet unemployment benefits.*

### CONTROLS

* Q : Clear Trangles (make sure there aren't too many trangles, it might crash your computer lol)
* R : Spawn moe Trangles

### How to Play

- There is no interaction in this simple simulator! Just watch.
- Trangles will multiply if they touch a like-colored trangle 3 times.
- Trangles will die if they touch 3 opposite-colored trangles.
- Trangles grow slightly over time.
