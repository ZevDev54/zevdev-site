*Feed the fire and protect it from evil snowmen to stay alive.*

### CONTROLS

* WASD : Move
* Mouse : Aim
* Space/left click : Charge/throw snowball
* F : Place turret
* G : Place wall

### How to Play

- Gather firewood from the surrounding trees
- Move close to the campfire, and the wood will be put on to the fire automatically.
- Use 3 wood to place a turret near your fire, which will kill the enemy snowmen
- Use 2 wood to place a wall around your fire, to protect it from enemy snowballs
