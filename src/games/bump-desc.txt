*Bump your friends to their doom!*

### CONTROLS

* WASD : P1 (blue) movement
* Arrowkeys : P2 (red) movement
* TFGH : P3 (green) movement
* IJKL : P4 (yellow) movement
* Number keys (1-9) : Change to level

### How to Play

- Bump into your friends and knock them into the purple.
- Everyone except the person who dies gains a point

### Reflection

* The first local-multiplayer game I made.
* With more interesting graphics, this would be a really neat little game!
